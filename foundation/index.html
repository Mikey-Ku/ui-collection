Nothing on this page was invented. Every value is either taken directly from the site's tokens.css or derived from a rule its components already follow. White paper, cool near-black ink, one restrained accent, 4px radius, and things that lift rather than glow.

Colour

Paper and ink

Seven values carry the whole interface. There is no second hue — the accent is the only colour in the system, which is what makes it mean something when it appears.

Accent

One, swappable

Emerald is what the site ships. The site's own token file documents three alternates in a comment — they are wired to the swatches above, so the swap is something you can look at rather than imagine. Cobalt is the blue you were reaching for.

Section label

Body copy with a link in it, and the accent used as text.

Type

Space Grotesk & Inter

Display face at weight 500 with tight tracking; body at 400 with a generous 1.65 line height. The display face also runs the buttons and the section labels, which is what ties the chrome together.

Depth

Nothing rests raised

The site has no resting shadow anywhere. Surfaces sit flat inside a hairline and earn their elevation on hover — a long, soft, far-offset shadow rather than a tight dark one. Hover the right-hand card.

At rest

Hairline only. No shadow.

On hover

Lifts 4px, border darkens, shadow appears.

Motion

Expo-out, unhurried

One easing curve for everything, taken from the site. It starts fast and settles slowly, which is why the hover lift reads as physical rather than snappy. Durations step by role.

--mk-ease
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
--mk-dur-fast
0.25s — buttons, swatches, anything under the cursor
--mk-dur
0.4s — cards, panels, accordions
--mk-dur-slow
0.6s — images, reveals, anything large
